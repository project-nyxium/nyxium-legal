# Nyxium™ — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 7, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Service:** Nyxium™ Discord Bot

At Nyxium™, we respect your privacy and are committed to explaining how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you interact with our Discord bot and related services.

This Privacy Policy applies to users and Discord servers that use Nyxium™.

By using Nyxium™, you acknowledge the practices described in this Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

Nyxium™ may collect and process the following information depending on which features are enabled and used.

### 1.1 Discord Identifiers

- Discord User IDs.
- Discord Server (Guild) IDs.
- Channel IDs.
- Role IDs.
- Message IDs.
- Other Discord identifiers required for bot functionality.

These identifiers help Nyxium™ recognize users, servers, and resources when executing commands and providing features.

### 1.2 Server Configuration

We may store configuration information such as:

- Custom server prefixes.
- Moderation and Anti-Nuke settings.
- Auto Moderation configurations.
- Logging webhook configurations.
- Custom server settings.
- Autoresponders, tags, and sticky message configurations.
- Premium feature settings.

### 1.3 Moderation Information

When moderation or security features are used, Nyxium™ may process and store:

- Moderation case information.
- User and moderator IDs.
- Moderation action types.
- Reasons provided for moderation actions.
- Relevant timestamps.
- Information necessary for Anti-Nuke, Anti-Raid, or other security systems.

### 1.4 Command and Feature Information

Nyxium™ may process information submitted through commands and interactions to provide requested functionality.

Depending on the feature, this may include command arguments, message content, user selections, and other relevant information.

We do not intend to collect information unrelated to operating or maintaining the Service.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information processed by Nyxium™ may be used to:

- Operate bot commands and features.
- Maintain server configurations.
- Provide moderation and security functionality.
- Generate moderation and activity logs.
- Prevent abuse, spam, and exploitation.
- Verify Premium access and feature eligibility.
- Diagnose technical issues.
- Maintain and improve service reliability.
- Protect Nyxium™ and its users from unauthorized activity.

## 3. Data Storage and Retention

Information may be stored in databases, configuration files, or other storage systems used to operate Nyxium™.

Retention periods depend on the type of information and the feature involved.

- Server settings may remain stored while required for the relevant feature.
- Moderation records may be retained for administrative and security purposes.
- Temporary data may be automatically removed after its intended retention period.
- Certain records may remain after Nyxium™ leaves a server until they are deleted according to applicable retention procedures.

We do not guarantee that every type of information is deleted immediately when the bot is removed from a server.

## 4. Logging and Webhooks

Nyxium™ may send information to Discord webhook URLs configured by server administrators or the Nyxium™ operators.

These logs may contain:

- Command and moderation activity.
- Server and channel identifiers.
- User and moderator identifiers.
- Security events.
- Bot server join and removal events.
- Other information required by the configured logging feature.

Server administrators are responsible for managing their own webhook URLs and controlling who can access the channels where webhook messages are delivered.

Information sent through Discord webhooks is also subject to Discord's applicable policies.

## 5. Information Sharing

Nyxium™ does not intend to sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be processed or shared when necessary for:

- Operating Discord bot functionality.
- Delivering configured webhook logs.
- Using hosting providers and infrastructure services.
- Maintaining security and preventing abuse.
- Complying with legal obligations.
- Responding to legitimate security or technical incidents.

We do not grant unrelated third parties unrestricted access to stored information.

## 6. Third-Party Services

Nyxium™ relies on Discord and may use external hosting providers, APIs, databases, and other infrastructure services.

These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Nyxium™ is an independent service and is not officially affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Discord Inc.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Nyxium™.

These measures may include:

- Restricted access to bot infrastructure.
- Secure handling of configuration and credentials.
- Access controls for administrative systems.
- Measures to prevent unauthorized access or modification.
- Updates and maintenance to address security vulnerabilities.

However, no online service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Users and server administrators should never share Discord authentication tokens, passwords, or confidential credentials through bot commands or public channels.

## 8. User and Server Administrator Responsibilities

Users are responsible for the information they submit through Nyxium™.

Server administrators are responsible for:

- Configuring logging and moderation features.
- Controlling access to webhook destinations.
- Informing their communities about relevant server logging practices.
- Managing server-specific data and settings.
- Ensuring their use of Nyxium™ complies with applicable laws and Discord policies.

## 9. Data Requests and Deletion

Users and server administrators may contact the Nyxium™ team through its official support channels to request assistance regarding information associated with their accounts or servers.

Requests may require verification of ownership or administrative authority.

Some information may need to be retained where necessary for security, legitimate operational purposes, or legal obligations.

We will review legitimate requests and respond as reasonably possible.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Nyxium™ introduces new features, modifies existing systems, or changes its data-handling practices.

Updated versions will be published through official Nyxium™ communication channels.

Continued use of Nyxium™ after an updated Policy becomes effective constitutes acknowledgment of the revised Policy, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

## 11. Contact and Support

For privacy-related questions, concerns, or data requests, contact the Nyxium™ team through its official support channels.
